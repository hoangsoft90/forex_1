🚀 Getting Started

📋 Trading Constitution

Your personal set of trading rules. The app checks every trade against these rules and interrupts you before you break them.

How It Works

  • Create rules — tap "Add Rule" and choose from templates or write custom ones.
  • Set limits — each rule has a measurable threshold (e.g., "Max risk per trade: 2%").
  • Adaptive Rules — some rules auto-adjust based on market volatility. When ATR is high, the app suggests reducing your risk.
  • Decision Interruption — when you're about to break a rule, the app pops up a warning and asks you to confirm.

⚡ Fast Plan

Create a complete trade plan in under 15 seconds.

Steps

1 Select Symbol — choose from 37 supported pairs (EURUSD, XAUUSD, BTCUSD, US30, etc.)
2 Enter Entry Price — your planned entry point
3 Set Stop Loss — the app validates this against your rules
4 Set Risk % — how much of your balance you're willing to risk
5 Lot Size auto-calculated — the app does the math using forex-standard pip values

What You Get

  • Exact lot size based on your SL distance and risk %
  • Risk validation — if it violates your rules, the app blocks saving
  • TradingView chart integrated in the form for quick analysis
  • Take Profit suggestions based on R:R ratio
The lot size calculation works for all 37 supported symbols including forex majors, crosses, commodities (XAUUSD, XAGUSD), indices (US30, NAS100), and crypto (BTCUSD, ETHUSD).

📝 Journal & Delta

Log your trades and compare what you planned vs what actually happened.

Logging a Trade

2 Enter your actual entry, exit, and SL — or paste from MT4/MT5.
3 The app auto-matches it to your plan and calculates the delta.

📈 Cost of Indiscipline

The most powerful feature — see exactly how much money your rule-breaking costs you.

How It Works

The app calculates: "If you had followed your plan perfectly, how much would you have made?" Then it compares that to your actual result.

1 You enter a trade with a plan (entry, SL, TP, lot size).
2 You deviate from the plan (move SL, change lot, revenge trade).
3 The app shows: "Following plan: +$X. Actual result: -$Y. Cost: $Z."
This feature requires at least 30 logged trades with at least 3 plan deviations to generate meaningful data.
The hypothetical PnL is an estimate — it assumes you would have held to your TP. Real results may vary. Always check the disclaimer.

🔥 Discipline Streak

Count your consecutive trades that followed the plan. The longer your streak, the stronger your discipline muscle.

How Streaks Work

  • +1 when a trade is logged as "On Plan"
  • Reset to 0 when a trade is "Off Plan"
  • Streak is based on entry time, not when you logged the trade
  • Importing old trades won't break your current streak
Aim for a streak of 10+. Most profitable traders maintain streaks of 20+.

🎯 Personal Danger Zone

Discover when during the day you're most likely to break your rules.

What It Shows

  • Hourly heatmap — which hours have the most violations
  • Pattern detection — recurring behaviors like "always revenge trade after 3pm"
  • Timezone-aware — uses your profile timezone (not device timezone)

Using This Data

When the app detects a pattern (≥ 5 violations at the same time), it will:

  1. Show a warning before you trade during danger hours
  2. Suggest taking a break during high-risk periods
  3. Track whether your danger zone improves over time

📊 Setup Analytics

Group your trades by setup type and discover which strategies actually have an edge.

How It Works

  • Tag each trade with a setup type (e.g., "Breakout", "Pullback", "News")
  • The app calculates win rate, average R:R, and edge for each setup
  • Requires ≥ 30 trades per setup tag for statistical significance

Reading the Results

MetricWhat It Means
Win Rate% of trades that hit TP before SL
Average R:RMean reward-to-risk ratio
Edge ScoreCombined score — positive = profitable edge
Sample SizeNumber of trades — more = more reliable
If a setup has a negative edge after 30+ trades, consider removing it from your strategy.

❓ Frequently Asked Questions

Is my data private?

Yes. Your trading data is protected by Row Level Security — only you can see it. We never sell or share your data with third parties.

Which symbols are supported?

37 symbols including:

  • Forex Majors: EURUSD, GBPUSD, AUDUSD, NZDUSD, USDJPY, USDCAD, USDCHF
  • Forex Crosses: EURJPY, GBPJPY, EURGBP, EURAUD, and more
  • Commodities: XAUUSD (Gold), XAGUSD (Silver), USOIL, UKOIL
  • Indices: US30, NAS100, SPX500, DE40, UK100
  • Crypto: BTCUSD, ETHUSD, BNBUSD, SOLUSD

Can I import trades from MT4/MT5?

Yes! Copy your trade history from MetaTrader (tab-separated format) and paste it into the app. The parser auto-detects columns and imports your trades.

Does the app work offline?

Core features (logging, viewing trades) work offline. Rules sync, danger zone analysis, and cloud backup require an internet connection.

How do I change language?

Go to Settings → Language and choose between English and Vietnamese. The app auto-detects your device language on first launch.

What is the Discipline Score?

A 0–100 score calculated daily based on: plan adherence, risk compliance, rule violations, and trade quality. Higher = more disciplined.

Can I delete my account?

Yes. Go to Settings → Account → Delete Account. All your data is permanently removed from our servers.
